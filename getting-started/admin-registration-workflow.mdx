# Administrator first steps

Administrator actions can change other people's access. First check the school and plan shown on the dashboard, then make only the change you need.

<img src="/images/diagrams/admin-operations-map.svg" alt="Administrator workflow: review subscription capacity, manage users and sections, then verify access and activity." />

## Sign in

Open the Chanakya AI portal and enter the Admin User Id and password given by your school. Everyone uses the same sign-in page; your role decides which area opens.

<img src="/images/app/student/sign-in.png" alt="Chanakya AI sign-in page with User Id and password fields." />

Do not save an Admin password on a shared device.

## 1. Review the dashboard

Check:

- Total students, teachers, and sections.
- The selected plan.
- Used and available accounts.
- Renewal or expiry information.
- Any warning that needs attention.

If more than one plan appears, choose the correct one before checking its limits.

<img src="/images/app/admin/dashboard-summary.png" alt="Administrator dashboard subscription context and summary cards for Students, Teachers, Sections, and remaining subscription time." />

## 2. Search before creating

Use **Students** or **Teachers** to search by the person's User Id and name. Make sure they do not already have an account.

Duplicate accounts can split a person's history, use an extra account space, and cause class-assignment problems.

## 3. Verify identity before changing access

Before editing, resetting a password, or removing an account, confirm:

- The User Id.
- The school.
- The class and subject.
- The intended section.
- Whether active work depends on the account.

<Warning>
  Never share a user's password. Follow your school's identity-check and password-reset process.
</Warning>

## 4. Maintain sections

Sections connect students and teachers to the right classes and work. After changing membership, check the list and ask the person to sign out and back in.

Before deleting a section, check for active tests, homework, and historical reporting that may depend on it.

## 5. Review activity

Use **Activity Logs** to check a recent change. Search by time and account, then compare the log with what you see now.

## Mobile navigation

On a phone, select the menu button at the top to open **Dashboard**, **Tests**, **Homework**, **Students**, **Teachers**, **Sections**, and **Help**.

# Administrator first steps

Administrator actions affect other people's access. Start by confirming the organization and subscription context, then make the smallest necessary change.

<img src="/images/diagrams/admin-operations-map.svg" alt="Administrator workflow: review subscription capacity, manage users and sections, then verify access and activity." />

## 1. Review the dashboard

Check:

- Total students, teachers, and sections.
- The selected subscription.
- Used and available capacity.
- Renewal or expiry information.
- Any warning that needs attention.

If multiple subscriptions appear, select the intended one before interpreting limits.

## 2. Search before creating

Use **Students** or **Teachers** to search by the provided identifier and name. Confirm that the person does not already have an account.

Duplicate accounts can split history, consume capacity, and create confusing section membership.

## 3. Verify identity before changing access

Before editing, resetting a password, or removing an account, confirm:

- The unique account identifier.
- The organization.
- The class or teaching context.
- The intended section.
- Whether active work depends on the account.

<Warning>
  Never publish or share a user's password. Follow your organization's approved identity-verification and password-reset process.
</Warning>

## 4. Maintain sections

Sections connect students and teachers to the correct academic workflows. After a membership change, verify the roster and ask the affected user to sign out and back in.

Before deleting a section, check for active tests, homework, and historical reporting that may depend on it.

## 5. Review activity

Use **Activity Logs** when you need to investigate a recent operational change. Narrow the review to the relevant time and account, then compare the event with the current record.

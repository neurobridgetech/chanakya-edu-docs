# Teacher first steps

Your organization creates your teacher account and assigns your sections and subjects. After sign-in, confirm those assignments before creating student work.

<img src="/images/diagrams/teacher-assessment-lifecycle.svg" alt="Teacher assessment lifecycle from creation and validation through assignment, review, publication, and instructional follow-up." />

## 1. Confirm your teaching context

On **Dashboard**, check the class and subject selectors. The cards, upcoming work, performers, and chapter analysis use the selected context.

<img src="/images/app/teacher-dashboard.png" alt="Teacher dashboard showing class and subject selectors, summary cards, upcoming tests, and performance insights." />

<Note>
  The values in screenshots are examples from a demonstration account. Your classes, subjects, dates, and metrics will differ.
</Note>

## 2. Review your workspace

The primary navigation can include:

- **Dashboard** for class activity and performance.
- **Tests** for test creation, status, submissions, and analysis.
- **Homework** for assignment workflows.
- **Sections** for assigned class groups and rosters.
- **Help** for assistance.

Features appear only when your organization enables them.

## 3. Prepare your first test

Open **Tests** and select **New Test**. Choose between creating a test from curriculum selections and uploading an existing paper.

<img src="/images/app/teacher-create-test.png" alt="Create Test form with Create Test and Upload Paper modes, class, section, subject, question count, date, and chapter fields." />

Before submitting the form:

- Select the intended class and section.
- Choose the subject and curriculum scope.
- Confirm the question count and date.
- Use a recognizable test name.
- Review any advanced options.

## 4. Preview before students receive it

Check question text, choices, answer keys, marks, diagrams, mathematical notation, optional groups, total marks, and instructions. Uploaded papers require the same review after extraction.

<Warning>
  Do not assign an uploaded paper while it is still processing or after extraction has failed.
</Warning>

## 5. Monitor and publish

Use **View students** to monitor submission status. Review grading and any re-checks before publishing results. Students can see results only after the configured publication step completes.

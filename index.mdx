## Sign in

1. Open the [Chanakya AI portal](https://edu.aichanakya.in/).
2. Enter the **User Id** provided by your school or organization.
3. Enter your password and select **Login**.
4. Chanakya AI opens the workspace for your assigned role.

<Warning>
  Never share your password in screenshots, support requests, or documentation. Ask your administrator to reset access when you cannot sign in.
</Warning>

## How access works

Your role and your school's setup decide what you can see. Some features may not appear in every account. If something is missing, ask your teacher or administrator to check your access.

| Role | Main responsibilities | Best place to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student | Complete assigned work and learn from feedback. | [Student first steps](/getting-started/student-onboarding-workflow) |
| Teacher | Create, review, assign, grade, publish, and analyze work. | [Teacher first steps](/getting-started/teacher-registration-workflow) |
| Administrator | Maintain organization access, capacity, accounts, and sections. | [Administrator first steps](/getting-started/admin-registration-workflow) |

## Common navigation

- Use the main menu to move between your dashboard and your work.
- Open the profile menu to access **Settings**, submit feedback, or log out.
- On a phone, use the menu and bottom navigation to find the same main areas.
- A maintenance notice or release update can appear after sign-in. Follow the message shown in the app.

<Tip>
  New to the platform? Follow the [Quickstart](/quickstart), then open the guide for your role.
</Tip>

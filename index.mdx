## Sign in

1. Open the [Chanakya AI portal](https://edu-platform-frontend.pages.dev/).
2. Enter the **User Id** provided by your school or organization.
3. Enter your password and select **Login**.
4. Chanakya AI opens the workspace for your assigned role.

<Warning>
  Never share your password in screenshots, support requests, or documentation. Ask your administrator to reset access when you cannot sign in.
</Warning>

## How access works

Your role and your organization's enabled features determine which navigation items appear. Some workspaces may not include every feature described in these guides. Tests, homework, sections, user management, and resources can also depend on the organization's active subscription.

## Common navigation

- Use the primary navigation to move between your dashboard and role-specific work.
- Open the profile menu to access **Settings**, submit feedback, or log out.
- On a phone, use the compact navigation controls; the same core workflows remain available.
- A maintenance notice or release update can appear after sign-in. Follow the message shown in the app.

<Tip>
  Start with the overview for your role, then open the task-specific page when you need step-by-step guidance.
</Tip>

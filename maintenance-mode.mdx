# Maintenance Mode

Chanakya AI may sometimes be unavailable while we make updates or fixes. Your saved data remains protected.

<img src="/images/diagrams/maintenance-window.svg" alt="Before maintenance, finish active work and plan offline; during maintenance, wait; after maintenance, sign in and verify important work." />

## Before maintenance

A warning banner can show when maintenance starts and ends. The app works normally until maintenance begins.

Students should submit work in progress, download any needed materials, and plan offline study if needed.

Teachers should tell students, check affected deadlines, download important materials, and prepare an alternative activity if needed.

## During maintenance

During maintenance, the usual app may be replaced by a maintenance screen. You cannot use the app until service returns.

Maintenance can include regular updates, urgent fixes, or new features. The time needed depends on the work.

## After maintenance

When service returns, sign in and continue from your dashboard. Your assignments, submissions, grades, feedback, and settings should still be there.

## If maintenance affects an assessment or deadline

Students should tell their teacher if a test or deadline was affected. Teachers can decide on an extension, a new session, or another option.

## Time shown in the notice

If a notice uses **UTC**, it is a worldwide time standard. For example, 02:00 UTC is 07:30 in India.

## Troubleshooting after maintenance

If the platform is still unavailable after the stated window:

1. Check the internet connection.
2. Refresh the page.
3. Try another supported browser or device.
4. Allow a short period for all services to restart.
5. Contact your teacher, school office, or support contact if you still cannot sign in.

If work appears to be missing, check for saved drafts and contact the relevant teacher or support contact with details of what happened.

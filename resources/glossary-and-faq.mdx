## Glossary and frequently asked questions

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | A person who manages school accounts, sections, access, plan use, and operational activity. |
| **Analysis** | Information created from marked work, such as question patterns, strengths, weak areas, and suggested next actions. |
| **Answer key** | The expected answers used by authorized staff when checking a paper. |
| **Assessment** | A general term for a Test, Homework, or another piece of assigned work. |
| **Batch** | An older product term for a section. Public guides use **section**. |
| **Class analysis** | A Teacher view that combines overall, question, and Student evidence for one assessment. |
| **Exercise** | An internal or older label that can appear in routes or system messages. The main navigation uses **Tests**. |
| **Homework** | Take-home work with its own availability and submission rules. |
| **Marking guide** | Instructions used to decide how marks should be awarded. |
| **Published** | Reviewed results are available to Students. |
| **Re-check** | A new review of a submission or question after a possible marking or evidence problem. |
| **Result** | The marks, feedback, and available analysis for submitted work. |
| **Section** | A teaching group that connects Students, Teachers, subjects, Tests, and Homework. |
| **Submission** | A Student's saved or uploaded response to assigned work. |
| **Test** | Assigned assessment work shown in the **Tests** area. |
| **User Id** | The sign-in identifier provided by the school. It may not be an email address. |

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does my workspace look different from a screenshot?

Your role, school plan, assigned classes, enabled features, device size, and available data affect what appears. Screenshots use example data.

### Why is a menu item missing?

The feature may not be enabled for your school or role. Sign out and sign in again after an access change, then ask a Teacher or Administrator to verify your assignment.

### Why is a feature locked?

Some Tests, Homework, Resources, or management features depend on the school plan. Contact an Administrator when the app asks you to enable access.

### Why can I not see my result?

Submission processing, marking, analysis, or Teacher review may still be incomplete. Students see results after the Teacher publishes them.

### Can I upload images and a PDF together?

No. Use one PDF or the allowed image files. Remove the current selection before switching formats.

### What happens when I re-upload Homework?

The new answer sheet replaces the earlier one after processing. Confirm that the replacement is complete and belongs to the correct Homework.

### Who can correct my class or section?

Tell your Teacher first if Student work is missing. An Administrator manages account and section assignments.

### Does Analysed mean Students can see the result?

Not always. **Analysed** means analysis is ready for staff review. **Published** means the Student can see the shared result.

### Should I create another account when I cannot sign in?

No. Ask an Administrator to verify or reset the existing account. A duplicate account can separate assignments and results.

### What should I send to support?

Send your role, page, action, status or error, approximate time, and a short description. Never send a password, full answer sheet, or complete Student list.

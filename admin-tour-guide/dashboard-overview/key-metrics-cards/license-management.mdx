# License management

Select the active subscription to review resource utilization. Fixed-capacity resources show used and total values; unlimited resources show current usage without a hard limit.

<img src="/images/app/admin/usage-and-quotas.png" alt="Subscription usage cards and quota table with used, limit, and remaining values." />

## Before adding accounts

- Confirm the correct subscription.
- Check remaining student and teacher capacity.
- Review the renewal date or warning.
- Remove or correct duplicate accounts before requesting more capacity.

Contact your Chanakya AI account representative when legitimate usage is approaching a fixed limit.

## Interpret quota rows

- **Used** is current consumption.
- **Limit** is the subscription maximum.
- **Remaining** is the available capacity.
- A zero limit can mean the resource is not included; confirm with your subscription contact before assuming it is unlimited.

Check the dashboard's last-updated time when reconciling a recent change.

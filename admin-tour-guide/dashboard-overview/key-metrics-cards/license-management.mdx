## License management

Select the active plan to review what is in use. Fixed limits show used and total values. Unlimited resources show use without a maximum.

<img src="/images/app/admin/usage-and-quotas.png" alt="Subscription usage cards and quota table with used, limit, and remaining values." />

## Before adding accounts

- Confirm the correct plan.
- Check how many student and teacher accounts remain.
- Review the renewal date or warning.
- Remove or correct duplicate accounts before requesting more capacity.

Contact your Chanakya AI account contact when you are close to a fixed limit.

## Read the plan table

- **Used** is how much is in use now.
- **Limit** is the plan maximum.
- **Remaining** is the space still available.
- A zero limit can mean the plan does not include that resource. Check with your plan contact before assuming it is unlimited.

Check the dashboard's last-updated time when reconciling a recent change.

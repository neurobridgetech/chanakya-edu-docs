## Teacher overview

Search for the teacher, check the school account, and review section or subject assignments. Open Teacher management to make changes.

<img src="/images/app/admin/teacher-list-columns.png" alt="Teacher list headings for identity, assigned teaching context, account identifiers, and actions." />

After updating assignments, ask the teacher to sign out and back in if the expected navigation does not refresh.

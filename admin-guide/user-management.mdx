# Manage users

Use **Students** and **Teachers** to search, create, and maintain organization accounts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search first">
    Search by the provided identifier and name. Confirm that the person does not already have an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check capacity">
    Review the active subscription before adding a licensed account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or edit">
    Enter the required identity and academic details, then assign the correct class, section, or teaching context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify access">
    Confirm the account appears in the list and the intended section membership is correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password resets and removal

Reset a password only for the verified account. Remove an account only when organizational policy permits it and active work no longer depends on it.

<Warning>
  Never send passwords through public documentation, screenshots, or shared support channels.
</Warning>

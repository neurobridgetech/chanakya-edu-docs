# Manage users

Use **Students** and **Teachers** to search, create, and maintain organization accounts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search first">
    Search by the provided identifier and name. Confirm that the person does not already have an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check capacity">
    Review the active subscription before adding a licensed account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or edit">
    Enter the required identity and academic details, then assign the correct class, section, or teaching context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify access">
    Confirm the account appears in the list and the intended section membership is correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password resets and removal

Reset a password only for the verified account. Remove an account only when organizational policy permits it and active work no longer depends on it.

<Warning>
  Never send passwords through public documentation, screenshots, or shared support channels.
</Warning>

## Student account checklist

- Confirm the student's unique identifier and name.
- Choose the intended class and section.
- Check for an existing record with a similar identifier.
- Verify licensed student capacity.
- Confirm the account appears in the correct roster.

## Teacher account checklist

- Confirm the teacher's unique identifier and organization.
- Verify licensed teacher capacity.
- Assign the intended sections and subjects.
- Ask the teacher to sign out and back in after assignment changes.
- Confirm that navigation shows only the enabled role features.

## Correcting a duplicate

Do not delete either account immediately. First determine which record contains active submissions, grading history, or assignments. Follow your organization's approved correction process so academic history is not lost.

## Troubleshooting access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The account exists but the user cannot sign in">
    Verify the User Id exactly, check account status, and follow the approved password-reset process. Never ask the user to send you their existing password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The user signs in but sees the wrong tools">
    Confirm the role, enabled subscription features, and section or subject assignments. Ask the user to sign out and back in after corrections.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The user is missing from a section">
    Open the section, add the intended account, save, and recheck the roster. Confirm that you changed the correct organization record.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

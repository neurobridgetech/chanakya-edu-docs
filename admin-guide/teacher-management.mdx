# Manage Teacher accounts

Open **Teachers** to review teaching accounts. The page provides **Add Teacher** and **Export** actions.

<img src="/images/app/admin/teacher-list-actions.png" alt="Manage Teachers heading with Add Teacher and Export actions." />

## Search and filter

Search by name, mobile number, or email. Use the section filter to narrow the list when a teacher has several assignments.

<img src="/images/app/admin/teacher-list-filters.png" alt="Teacher search and section filter." />

The list separates the teacher's name, assigned class and subject context, mobile or username, email, and actions.

<img src="/images/app/admin/teacher-list-columns.png" alt="Teacher table headings for name, assignments, account identifiers, email, and actions." />

## Add a Teacher

Select **Add Teacher** and enter the required name and phone number. Email is optional.

<img src="/images/app/admin/create-teacher.png" alt="Create Teacher form with name, phone number, optional email, and submit action." />

Creating the account and assigning teaching context are separate responsibilities. After creation, verify the intended sections and subjects before asking the teacher to begin work.

## Assignment checklist

- Confirm the correct board and class.
- Confirm every intended section.
- Assign only the subjects the teacher owns.
- Remove stale assignments when responsibility changes.
- Ask the teacher to sign out and back in.
- Confirm the expected Tests, Homework, and Sections navigation.

## Edit, reset, or remove

Use the actions column to edit, reset a password, or delete. Verify the account using the approved identity process before a reset. Before removal, check owned assessments, pending grading, section relationships, and reporting requirements.

<Warning>
  Removing a Teacher account can affect access to assessment workflows. Reassign responsibility before removal when active work still depends on that teacher.
</Warning>

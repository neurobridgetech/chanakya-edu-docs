## Safe administration checklist

1. Confirm the organization and subscription context.
2. Search for an existing record before creating one.
3. Check license capacity.
4. Verify the target user's unique identifier.
5. Confirm section and subject assignments.
6. Review activity after high-impact changes.

<Warning>
  Password resets, deletions, and membership changes affect another person's access. Confirm the exact account and intended outcome before saving.
</Warning>

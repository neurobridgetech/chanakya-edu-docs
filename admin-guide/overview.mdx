# Administrator guide

Administrators manage the school setup and access that teachers and students use. Use the Admin area to check available accounts, manage people and classes, check tests, and confirm important changes.

<img src="/images/diagrams/admin-operations-map.svg" alt="Administrator workflow: review subscription capacity, manage users and sections, then verify access and activity." />

## Your workspace

The main menu contains **Dashboard**, **Tests**, **Homework**, **Students**, **Teachers**, **Sections**, and **Help**. What you see can depend on your school's plan and your access.

## Admin responsibility map

| Area | Administrator owns | Verify afterward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Area | What you manage | Check after changing it |
| Plan | Available accounts, limits, and renewal date | The correct plan and remaining space |
| People | Create, correct, reset, and remove accounts | One correct account with the right access |
| Sections | Board, class, student list, and teacher subjects | Students, teachers, and subjects are connected |
| Tests and homework | Check and recover school work | Status, submissions, and reports still look right |
| Activity | Check recent changes | The log matches what you see now |

## High-impact actions

Password resets, account deletion, section deletion, and permanent assessment deletion can affect another person's access or history. Before using one:

1. Confirm the exact target.
2. Check whether active work or reports could be affected.
3. Use the smallest change that solves the problem.
4. Save once.
5. Reopen the record and review activity.

<Warning>
  Never place passwords, answer sheets, private contact details, or full activity-log records in public support messages or screenshots.
</Warning>

Read [Privacy and data handling](/resources/privacy-and-data-handling) before sharing screenshots, exports, account details, or support evidence.

## On a phone

Select the menu button at the top to open Admin navigation. For long lists or detailed settings, use a larger screen before saving.

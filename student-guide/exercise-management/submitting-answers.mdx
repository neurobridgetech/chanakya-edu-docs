# Submit your answers

Use the question controls to move through the paper. Depending on the question, you may type an answer, choose an option, or upload handwritten work.

<img src="/images/diagrams/student-submission-safety.svg" alt="Four-step submission checklist: prepare, upload, review, and confirm." />

## Review the paper

The top of the paper shows the title, class, subject, time, and total marks. Read the instructions and check how many questions you must answer in each section.

<img src="/images/app/student/paper-overview.png" alt="Assessment paper header with title, class, subject, time, and maximum marks." />

<img src="/images/app/student/paper-instructions.png" alt="General assessment instructions shown before the questions." />

Questions can contain text, images, choices, sub-questions, or longer written responses. Check the marks and required attempts before deciding how much time to spend.

<img src="/images/app/student/paper-question.png" alt="Example multiple-choice question in the Student paper view." />

## Save work as you go

Move between questions using the controls in the paper. Use saved or completion signs to find unfinished work. Do not refresh while uploading or submitting.

## Upload handwritten work

- Use bright, even lighting.
- Keep the entire answer inside the image.
- Avoid blur, shadows, and rotated pages.
- Upload pages in the intended order.
- Reopen the preview to confirm the image is readable.
- Use the file type requested by the dialog. A test can request a single PDF; homework can allow one PDF or multiple images.

<img src="/images/app/student/upload-and-submit.png" alt="Final test submission dialog with PDF selection and Upload and Complete action." />

For homework, a re-upload replaces the earlier answer sheet after the new file is ready.

<img src="/images/app/student/reupload-answer-sheet.png" alt="Homework re-upload dialog explaining accepted files and replacement processing." />

## Final review

Before selecting the final submit action:

1. Visit every question.
2. Check that required responses are present.
3. Verify selected options.
4. Open each uploaded image.
5. Confirm the deadline has not passed.
6. Submit once and wait for the confirmation screen.

## After submission

Check that the work shows **Submitted** or **Completed**. The result may appear later, after marking and teacher review.

<img src="/images/app/student/submission-confirmation.png" alt="Submission complete screen confirming that answers are saved and offering result or list actions." />

If the page stops responding during final submission:

1. Keep the tab open.
2. Wait briefly for the confirmation.
3. Check your connection.
4. Open the activity list in a separate step only after the request finishes.
5. Contact your teacher if the status remains uncertain.

<Warning>
  Do not close the page straight after submitting. Wait until Chanakya AI confirms that your work was submitted.
</Warning>

# Submit your answers

Use the question navigation to move through the paper. The interface may support typed responses, selected choices, or uploaded handwritten work depending on the question.

## Upload handwritten work

- Use bright, even lighting.
- Keep the entire answer inside the image.
- Avoid blur, shadows, and rotated pages.
- Upload pages in the intended order.
- Reopen the preview to confirm the image is readable.

## Final review

Before selecting the final submit action:

1. Visit every question.
2. Check that required responses are present.
3. Verify selected options.
4. Open each uploaded image.
5. Confirm the deadline has not passed.
6. Submit once and wait for the confirmation screen.

<Warning>
  Do not close the page immediately after submitting. Wait until Chanakya AI confirms that the attempt has ended or the submission status has changed.
</Warning>

# Access a test or exercise

1. Open the assessment or exercise list from the main navigation.
2. Locate the item by title, subject, or status.
3. Confirm the start time and deadline.
4. Open the item and read all instructions.
5. Select the available start or continue action.

## Understand the action shown

| Action or state | What it usually means |
| --- | --- |
| **Start** | The activity is available and you have not started it. |
| **Continue** | An attempt is already in progress. |
| **Submitted** | Your final submission was recorded. |
| **View result** | A graded result has been published. |
| No action | The activity may be early, late, disabled, or waiting for another stage. |

## Before starting

- Charge your device or connect it to power.
- Use a reliable internet connection.
- Close unrelated tabs and apps.
- Keep paper, a pen, or image-upload materials ready when required.
- Allow enough uninterrupted time before the deadline.

## If you cannot start

The item may be outside its availability window, already submitted, disabled by your organization, or blocked by subscription state. Refresh once, then contact your teacher if the expected action still does not appear.

When asking for help, include the activity title, subject, expected start time, and the message or state you see. Do not include your password.

<Warning>
  Do not open multiple copies of the same active attempt in different tabs or devices. Keep one attempt open to reduce the risk of conflicting progress.
</Warning>

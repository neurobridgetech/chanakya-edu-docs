# Use AI recommendations

Suggestions can recommend topics, question types, or study actions from your results.

<img src="/images/app/student/result-recommendations.png" alt="Personalized recommendations with progress and focused practice actions." />

## Recommended approach

1. Read the recommendation with the related result open.
2. Confirm that the cited weak area appears in your question feedback.
3. Choose a small, specific action.
4. Complete practice or ask your teacher for help.
5. Revisit the recommendation after new graded work.

## A useful recommendation is specific

Prefer “practice solving two-step linear equations and show every step” over “improve mathematics.” Turn broad suggestions into a small task that you can complete and check.

## When a recommendation looks wrong

Open the related result and check the questions behind it. A suggestion can be less useful when only a few tests have been marked or recent work is not ready.

Ask your teacher when:

- The recommendation conflicts with the current syllabus.
- It references a topic you were not assessed on.
- The shown result does not support the suggestion.
- You need help choosing appropriate practice.

<Warning>
  Suggestions can help you study, but they do not replace your teacher. Ask your teacher if a suggestion conflicts with class instructions or your result.
</Warning>

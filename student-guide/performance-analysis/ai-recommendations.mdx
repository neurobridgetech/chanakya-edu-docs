# Use AI recommendations

Recommendations can suggest topics, question types, or study actions based on available results.

## Recommended approach

1. Read the recommendation with the related result open.
2. Confirm that the cited weak area appears in your question feedback.
3. Choose a small, specific action.
4. Complete practice or ask your teacher for help.
5. Revisit the recommendation after new graded work.

<Warning>
  Recommendations support your learning plan; they do not replace teacher guidance. Ask your teacher when a suggestion conflicts with class instructions or the shown result.
</Warning>

## Performance analysis

This page helps you move from “What was my score?” to “What should I do next?”

Use this sequence:

1. Review the overall outcome.
2. Find questions where marks were lost.
3. Group mistakes by topic or question type.
4. Read the available feedback.
5. Choose one or two focused actions.
6. Check improvement after the next assessment.

<Note>
  These results use marked work your teacher has shared. With only a few tests, patterns may change.
</Note>

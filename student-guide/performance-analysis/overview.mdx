# Performance analysis

Performance analysis helps you move from “What was my score?” to “What should I do next?”

Use this sequence:

1. Review the overall outcome.
2. Find questions where marks were lost.
3. Group mistakes by topic or question type.
4. Read the available feedback.
5. Choose one or two focused actions.
6. Check improvement after the next assessment.

<Note>
  Analytics are based on available graded and published work. A short history can produce limited or unstable trends.
</Note>

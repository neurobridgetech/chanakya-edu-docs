# Review a result

A result can include total score, percentage, marks by question, answer feedback, and topic-level strengths or weaknesses.

<img src="/images/diagrams/result-visibility-flow.svg" alt="A result becomes visible after submission, grading, teacher review, and publication." />

## Read in this order

1. Check the total score and assessment details.
2. Review outcome counters such as correct, incorrect, or unanswered items.
3. Open each question to compare your response with the available feedback.
4. Note where marks were lost.
5. Use the analysis and recommendation sections to plan revision.

## When to ask for review

Contact your teacher when an uploaded answer is unreadable in the result, the wrong response appears, marks do not match the shown rubric, or feedback does not address your submitted work.

Give your teacher:

- The assessment title.
- The question number.
- The mark or feedback you expected to understand.
- A short explanation of the issue.

Do not post your full answer sheet or account details in a public channel.

## Turn feedback into action

Write down one concept to review, one error pattern to avoid, and one practice task to complete. Check the next comparable result for improvement instead of judging progress from a single score.

<Info>
  Results may remain unavailable until grading is complete and your teacher publishes them.

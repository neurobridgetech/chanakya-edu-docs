## Use performance insights

Insights can show changes over time, strong and weak topics, question patterns, consistency, and completion.

<img src="/images/app/student/result-performance.png" alt="Question-type performance and error-pattern cards in a Student result." />

## Interpret carefully

- A trend is more reliable when it includes several comparable assessments.
- A weak topic means it deserves attention, not that you cannot learn it.
- A high score can still hide a repeated mistake.
- Compare results taken under similar conditions.
- Use question feedback to explain the chart.

## Make an action plan

Choose one weak concept, review the relevant class material, complete targeted practice, and check the next result for improvement.

Use this simple format:

| Question | Your note |
| --- | --- |
| What pattern do I see? | For example, low marks in multi-step algebra. |
| What may be causing it? | For example, skipping intermediate steps. |
| What will I do? | Review one example and complete focused practice. |
| How will I check? | Compare the next similar question or assessment. |

## What insights cannot tell you

A chart cannot explain illness, missing work, misunderstood instructions, or an unclear upload. Check the questions and ask your teacher about unusual results.

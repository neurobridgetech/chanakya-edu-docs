# Review questions and feedback

Open a published result and select **Questions**. The summary shows how many questions earned full marks, need review, or were not attempted.

<img src="/images/app/student/question-review-summary.png" alt="Question review summary with full-marks, review, and not-attempted counts." />

## Read a question card

Each card shows:

- Question number and type.
- Marks earned and maximum marks.
- Chapter.
- Submission state.
- A short question preview.

<img src="/images/app/student/question-review-card.png" alt="Question card with type, marks, chapter, submitted state, and Review answer action." />

Select **Review answer** to expand the details.

## Compare the evidence

Read the expanded view in this order:

1. **Question** — confirm what was asked.
2. **Your Answer** — verify the response that was graded.
3. **Correct Answer** — compare the expected response.
4. **Explanation** — understand why marks were gained or lost.
5. **Next Actions** — choose one practice step.

<img src="/images/app/student/question-feedback.png" alt="Expanded question feedback showing the question, student answer, correct answer, explanation, and next actions." />

<Note>
  A not-attempted question can still appear in the review list. It has no submitted response to compare, but the question and available guidance can still support revision.
</Note>

## Ask for a review

Contact your teacher when the response shown is not what you submitted, an uploaded page is unreadable, the marks conflict with the displayed feedback, or the explanation does not address your work.

Include the assessment title and question number. Do not send your password or post your full answer sheet publicly.

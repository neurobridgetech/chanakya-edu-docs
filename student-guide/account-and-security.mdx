# Manage your Student account

Open the account menu from your name or the mobile menu, then select **Settings**. The page separates **Profile** and **Security**.

## Profile

Profile shows details from your school, such as your name, class, and date of birth. Tell your teacher or administrator if a locked detail is wrong.

## Security

Use **Security** to change your password or sign out.

<img src="/images/app/student/account-security.png" alt="Student Security settings with password fields, password requirements, and sign-out guidance." />

To change your password:

1. Enter the current password.
2. Enter a new password with at least eight characters.
3. Include uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character.
4. Confirm the new password.
5. Select **Update Password** once.

<Warning>
  Never share your password with classmates, teachers, or support staff. Real support will not ask for it.
</Warning>

## Shared devices

- Sign out when you finish.
- Do not let the browser save the password on a shared device.
- Close the browser after signing out.
- Change the password immediately if someone else may know it.
- Tell your teacher or administrator if you cannot regain access.

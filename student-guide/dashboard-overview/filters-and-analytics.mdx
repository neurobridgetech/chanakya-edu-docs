# Choose a subject

Use the subject selector to focus the dashboard on one subject. Choose **All subjects** to see everything again.

A filter can change:

- Overall progress.
- Test and score summaries.
- Upcoming work for that subject.
- Study suggestions.

## Use filters well

- Compare one subject at a time when planning revision.
- Return to **All subjects** before checking your overall workload.
- Do not compare percentages from different subjects as if they came from the same tests.
- Open a result when you want to see individual questions.

<Tip>
  Use the subject view to choose what to study, then use the combined view to plan when to do it.
</Tip>

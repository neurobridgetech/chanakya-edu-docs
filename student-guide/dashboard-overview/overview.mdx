# Student dashboard

The dashboard is your starting point after sign-in. It turns your recent activity into a short plan for what to do next.

<img src="/images/app/student/dashboard.png" alt="Student dashboard with a subject filter, study progress, and the beginning of the Today plan." />

## Choose a subject

Use **Choose a subject** to select a class and subject. It can show both, such as **Science · 10th**. Select **View all** to see every subject again.

## Read your progress

The progress panel summarizes:

- Overall progress percentage.
- Tests completed.
- Average score.
- Upcoming tests and homework.

A zero can mean there are no marked results for that subject yet. It does not always mean something is missing.

<img src="/images/app/student/dashboard-progress.png" alt="Study progress panel with overall progress, completed tests, average score, and upcoming work." />

## Use the Today timeline

The timeline prioritizes:

1. The next test.
2. Upcoming homework.
3. A short study action based on current performance.

Each card contains a direct action such as **View tests**, **Open homework**, or **Start practice**. Select **View all** to open the wider list.

<img src="/images/app/student/dashboard-today.png" alt="Today plan with test, homework, and study-block cards." />

## Use the dashboard on a phone

On a phone, the progress and Today cards appear one below another. Use the bottom navigation for **Dashboard**, **Tests**, **Homework**, and **Help**. Use the top menu for your account options.

<img src="/images/app/student/dashboard-progress-mobile.png" alt="Mobile study progress panel with overall progress and summary statistics." />

<img src="/images/app/student/dashboard-today-mobile.png" alt="Mobile Today plan with vertically stacked student task cards." />

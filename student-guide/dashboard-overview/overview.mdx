# Student dashboard

The dashboard is your starting point after sign-in. It turns your recent activity into a short plan for what to do next.

## Choose a subject

Use the subject selector when it is available. **All subjects** shows the broad view; a specific subject narrows the progress and work context.

## Read your progress

The progress panel summarizes:

- Overall progress percentage.
- Tests completed.
- Average score.
- Upcoming tests and homework.

A zero value can mean that no graded work is available yet. It does not necessarily mean the feature is unavailable.

## Use the Today timeline

The timeline prioritizes:

1. The next test.
2. Upcoming homework.
3. A short study action based on current performance.

Select **View all** or the action on a timeline card to open the relevant list.

<Info>
  If the dashboard cannot refresh, your previous submissions remain safe. Check your connection, then select **Try again**.
</Info>

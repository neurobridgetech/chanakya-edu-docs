# Understand performance cards

Dashboard cards summarize data; they do not replace the detailed result for an individual assessment.

- **Overall progress** is a quick view of your results so far.
- **Tests completed** shows how many tests you have finished.
- **Average score** shows your marked work so far.
- **Upcoming work** shows future tests and homework.

## When a number looks unexpected

1. Confirm the selected subject.
2. Check whether the assessment has been graded.
3. Check whether results have been published.
4. Open the assessment result for the underlying marks.
5. Ask your teacher when the detailed result and dashboard disagree.

<Note>
  Recently submitted work can take time to appear while it is being marked or reviewed.
</Note>

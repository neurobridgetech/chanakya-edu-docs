# Student Guide

The Student Portal helps learners track academic progress, complete exercises and assessments, review results, and act on AI-generated recommendations.

## Dashboard overview

The dashboard is the central view for current performance, upcoming work, weak topics, and historical learning trends. Students can filter analytics by duration and subject to focus on a specific period or curriculum area.

Key dashboard cards include:

- **Total Exams** for quick access to examination history.
- **Performance Score** for the current overall academic percentage.
- **Weak Topics** for areas identified as needing more attention.
- **Upcoming Tests** for pending exams and exercises.

Analytics can include performance trends, subject comparisons, completion rates, milestones, and personalized recommendations.

## Exercise management

Exercises are topic-focused practice activities designed for skill development and reinforcement. They complement formal exams by allowing flexible practice and faster feedback.

Students can use exercises to:

- Practice specific chapters, topics, and concepts.
- Prepare for upcoming exams.
- Address weak areas identified by analytics.
- Track progress over repeated practice.
- Receive AI-assisted feedback on completed work.

Exercises can be accessed from the main navigation or dashboard. Students can review available work, open an exercise, complete required questions, and submit answers before the deadline.

## Submitting answers

Chanakya AI supports typed answers as well as uploaded handwritten work where enabled. Uploaded answer images are processed through OCR so written work can be evaluated digitally.

Before final submission, students should review all responses, confirm uploaded images are readable, check that required sections are complete, and verify the deadline. The platform records the submission time and updates the exercise status after completion.

Draft and resume behavior can preserve work during a session so students can continue without starting again.

## Reporting an issue

During an exercise or exam, students can report a problem with a question, answer option, submitted response, or explanation. A report can include a written description and is sent for administrative review without requiring the student to abandon the current session.

## Exercise results

Completed exercises provide a results view that can include:

- Overall score and percentage.
- Question-level correctness.
- Feedback on submitted answers.
- Identification of mistakes and strengths.
- Time and completion information.
- Historical progress across exercises.

## Performance analysis

Performance Insights turn assessment history into patterns that help students understand how they are learning. Analysis can identify strengths, improvement areas, trends over time, consistency, and relationships between subjects.

The system may also provide comparative analytics against a student's own historical performance, curriculum expectations, or aggregated peer benchmarks where configured.

## AI recommendations

AI-generated recommendations use exam and exercise performance to suggest next actions. Recommendations can focus on weak concepts, question types, repeated error patterns, practice priorities, and study strategies.

Analysis may include a strengths-and-weaknesses view, topic-level recommendations, question-type performance, and suggested exercises for targeted improvement.

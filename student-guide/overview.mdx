# Student guide

The student workspace brings today's work, overall progress, upcoming deadlines, completed results, and improvement guidance into one place.

<img src="/images/diagrams/student-workspace-map.svg" alt="Student workspace map from the dashboard to tests, homework, results, resources, and account settings." />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check today">
    Start on the dashboard. Review upcoming tests, homework, and the suggested study block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open assigned work">
    Use the main navigation to open tests or homework. Read the instructions and deadline before starting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete and review">
    Answer each question, attach clear handwritten work when requested, and review every response before final submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn from the result">
    After grading and publication, open the result to review marks, question feedback, strengths, and areas to improve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dashboard at a glance

- **Subject** selects the class and subject context for the dashboard.
- **Overall progress** summarizes the available results in that context.
- **Tests completed** shows completed assessment activity.
- **Average score** provides a quick performance indicator.
- **Upcoming work** combines pending tests and homework.
- **Today** prioritizes the next test, homework item, and a suggested study step.

<img src="/images/app/student/dashboard.png" alt="Student dashboard showing the subject selector, progress summary, and Today plan." />

## Navigation and availability

Use **Dashboard**, **Tests**, **Homework**, and **Help** in the main navigation. On a phone, the same destinations appear in the bottom navigation and the menu button opens account options.

Your school controls which features are enabled. Tests, homework, scheduled exams, image-to-text tools, and resources can vary by subscription and class assignment.

## What happens after submission

Submission is not always the final state. Chanakya AI can grade the attempt, allow teacher review or re-checking, and keep the result hidden until publication.

<img src="/images/diagrams/result-visibility-flow.svg" alt="A result becomes visible after submission, grading, teacher review, and publication." />

If a result is not visible, check the assessment status before assuming that the submission failed.

## Good assessment habits

- Read instructions before starting.
- Keep one attempt open on one device.
- Check every answer and attachment.
- Wait for a successful submission confirmation.
- Ask your teacher about a result only after noting the assessment title and the exact problem.
- Use feedback to choose a focused next action.

<Warning>
  Submit only when you are satisfied with every answer and attachment. Final submission can close the active attempt.
</Warning>

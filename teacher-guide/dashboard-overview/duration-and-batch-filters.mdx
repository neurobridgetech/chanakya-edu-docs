## Filter dashboard data

Choose the section and subject at the top of the dashboard. The cards and analysis update for your selection.

## Avoid common mistakes

- Confirm the section before discussing a student's result.
- Do not compare two sections without noting different assessment histories.
- Treat an empty subject view as “no results yet” until you check assignments.
- Open the related test or homework to check individual questions.

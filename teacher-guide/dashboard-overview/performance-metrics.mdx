## Interpret performance metrics

- **Class Average** shows marked work for the class and subject you selected.
- **Performance Trend** indicates movement across the displayed period.
- **Top Performers** highlights strong current outcomes.
- **Need Attention** lists students who may need a closer look.
- **Chapter Analysis** groups results by topic.

<Warning>
  Do not make an important decision about a student from one number. Check completed work, the difficulty of the test, and the student's answers first.
</Warning>

# Teacher guide

The Teacher workspace connects daily planning with assessment evidence. Start on **Dashboard**, create or upload work in **Tests** and **Homework**, monitor submissions, then use **Class analysis** to decide what to reteach.

<img src="/images/diagrams/teacher-workspace-map.svg" alt="Teacher workspace map connecting Dashboard to Tests and Homework, Class Analysis, Sections, and account tools." />

## Primary navigation

<img src="/images/app/teacher/mobile-navigation.png" alt="Teacher mobile navigation with Dashboard, Tests, Homework, Sections, and Help." />

- **Dashboard** — choose a section and subject, then scan workload and performance signals.
- **Tests** — create, upload, preview, filter, monitor, review, and analyze tests.
- **Homework** — upload assignments, set a duration, and follow the same submission and analysis workflow.
- **Sections** — view assigned sections, subjects, student counts, and rosters.
- **Help** — open support resources.
- **Profile menu** — open feedback, settings, or logout.

## The working cycle

<img src="/images/diagrams/teacher-assessment-lifecycle.svg" alt="Teacher assessment lifecycle from creation and validation through assignment, review, publication, and instructional follow-up." />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the teaching context">
    On **Dashboard**, choose the section and subject. Cards and insights update to that context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or upload">
    Use **New Test** for a generated test or uploaded paper. Use **New Homework** for a PDF assignment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate before release">
    Preview the paper, answer key, marks, dates, and assigned audience. AI-generated or extracted content requires teacher review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor and review">
    Open **View students** for submission status. Review learner evidence and re-check only when the underlying response justifies it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and act">
    After reviewing graded results, publish them and use **Class analysis** to plan reteaching, extension, or targeted practice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Publishing changes result visibility for learners. Confirm the assessment, grading evidence, and intended section before publishing.
</Warning>

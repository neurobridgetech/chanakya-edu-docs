title: "Comparative Analytics"
description: "Compare learners, sections, subjects, or periods with enough context to avoid misleading conclusions."
---

# Compare performance responsibly

Use comparable assessment scope, difficulty, marks, and time period. Note differences in completion and section size.

Good comparisons answer a specific question, such as:

- Did a section improve after reteaching?
- Which question caused difficulty across groups?
- Is a learner improving against their own earlier work?

Avoid ranking learners from a single assessment or comparing percentages built from different sets of work.

## Make a fair comparison

Record the assessment scope, total marks, completion rate, and number of learners. Compare the same measure across groups and state any difference in timing or difficulty.

| Better question | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Which concept improved after reteaching? | Which section is best? |
| Which question was difficult across both groups? | Who has the lowest percentage? |
| Is this learner improving against earlier comparable work? | Is this learner below everyone else? |

Use comparisons to choose instructional support, not to label learners.

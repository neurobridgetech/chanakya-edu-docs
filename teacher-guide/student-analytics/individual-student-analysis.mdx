# Analyze an individual learner

Open the learner from **View students** or the student analysis view.

Check:

- Submission and grading status.
- Total score and question-level marks.
- Typed or uploaded answer evidence.
- Feedback and rubric alignment.
- Re-check or manual-edit history where available.

Use inline mark editing or re-check only when the shown evidence supports a correction. Record the reason according to your organization's process.

## Review sequence

1. Confirm the learner and assessment.
2. Check whether the attempt was completed and graded.
3. Compare the answer evidence with the expected answer or rubric.
4. Review question-level marks before the total.
5. Check prior re-check or manual-edit state.
6. Save a correction only when the evidence supports it.
7. Reopen the result and verify the total.

<Warning>
  Do not infer understanding only from the total score. A learner may have missing work, an upload problem, or one high-impact error.
</Warning>

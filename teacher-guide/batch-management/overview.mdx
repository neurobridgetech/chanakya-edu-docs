# Sections

Open **Sections** to view the teaching groups assigned to your account.

<img src="/images/app/teacher/sections-overview.png" alt="Manage Sections heading with class and section filters." />

<img src="/images/app/teacher/sections-columns.png" alt="Section table columns for name, board, total students, subjects, and actions." />

Each row shows the section name, board, student count, assigned subjects, and **View Students** action.

<img src="/images/app/teacher/section-row-actions.png" alt="Example section row with subject assignments and View Students." />

## View the roster

<img src="/images/app/teacher/assigned-students-title.png" alt="Assigned Students dialog title for a section." />

<img src="/images/app/teacher/assigned-students-search.png" alt="Assigned Students search and learner count." />

<img src="/images/app/teacher/assigned-students-columns.png" alt="Assigned Students columns for name, roll number, and username." />

Use search to locate a learner. The roster is read-only in the Teacher workspace; contact an administrator if an assignment is incorrect.

<Warning>
  Rosters include names, roll numbers, and usernames. Do not publish or share roster screenshots outside authorized channels.
</Warning>

# Sections

Sections connect teachers, students, subjects, tests, and homework. Open **Sections** to see the class groups assigned to you.

Use sections to:

- Confirm learner membership.
- Navigate to the class roster.
- Scope dashboard and assessment filters.
- Ensure work is assigned to the intended group.

<Note>
  Teachers can only see sections and students permitted by their organization and assignments.
</Note>

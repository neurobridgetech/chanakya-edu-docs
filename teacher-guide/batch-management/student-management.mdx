## Review a section roster

The class list shows students in the section. Use it to check participation, open details when available, and investigate missing submissions.

Do not add, remove, or move accounts unless the option is available to you. Ask an administrator to correct class membership.

<Warning>
  Confirm both the learner and the section before reviewing or re-checking an assessment.
</Warning>

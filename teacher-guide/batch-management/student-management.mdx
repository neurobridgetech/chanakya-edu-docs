# Review a section roster

The roster shows learners assigned to the section. Use it to confirm participation, open learner details where available, and investigate missing submissions.

Teachers should not add, remove, or reassign organization accounts unless that control is explicitly available. Contact an administrator for membership corrections.

<Warning>
  Confirm both the learner and the section before reviewing or re-checking an assessment.
</Warning>

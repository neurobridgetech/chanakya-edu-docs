# View assigned sections

1. Open **Sections**.
2. Locate the section by class or section name.
3. Open the section to review its details and roster.
4. Confirm the subject and learner context before creating work.

If an expected section is missing, ask an administrator to verify your assignment rather than creating work for a different group.

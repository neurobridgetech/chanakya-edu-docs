# Create learning content

Teachers can create tests and homework from structured curriculum selections. Tests also support an **Upload Paper** mode when enabled.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Create from selections" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Choose class, section, subject, question count, date, and curriculum scope.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Upload a paper" icon="file-arrow-up">
    Upload an existing paper for extraction, then review the processing result.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Before you begin

Confirm:

- The intended class and section.
- The subject and curriculum scope.
- The expected question count and marks.
- The due date or availability window.
- Whether the work is a test or homework.
- Whether students should see results immediately after grading or only after publication.

## Safe publishing checklist

- Read every extracted or generated question.
- Verify answer keys, marks, and optional alternatives.
- Check images and mathematical notation.
- Confirm the selected students or sections.
- Check dates and instructions.
- Preview the student-facing paper.

<Warning>
  Do not publish an uploaded paper while its status is **Processing** or **Extraction Failed**. Review the completed extraction first, or correct the source file and upload it again.
</Warning>

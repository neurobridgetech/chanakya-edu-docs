# Preview and validate

From the Tests list, select **Preview**.

<img src="/images/app/teacher/preview-header.png" alt="Weekly Test Preview header with Back and Options actions." />

<img src="/images/app/teacher/preview-summary.png" alt="Test preview summary showing name, total questions, subject, total marks, and status." />

First confirm that the summary matches the intended assessment. Then inspect every section and question.

<img src="/images/app/teacher/preview-question.png" alt="Preview of a multiple-choice question with marks, options, type, and chapter." />

Check:

- section labels, total marks, and required attempts;
- question text, mathematical notation, diagrams, and ordering;
- option completeness and the correct option;
- question type, chapter, difficulty, and marks;
- answer-key explanations for factual or calculation errors.

## Preview options

<img src="/images/app/teacher/preview-options.png" alt="Preview Options menu for downloading the uploaded paper, exporting the question paper, and exporting the answer key." />

The available options can include the uploaded source, question-paper export, and answer-key export. Store answer keys separately and share them only with authorized staff.

<Note>
  Preview is the teacher’s quality gate. If anything is unclear or incorrect, edit or regenerate before learners start.
</Note>

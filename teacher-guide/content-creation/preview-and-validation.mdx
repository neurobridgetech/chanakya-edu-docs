# Preview and validate

Preview the paper after generation or extraction and before students receive it.

## Review checklist

- Title, subject, section, date, and instructions.
- Question order, numbering, and marks.
- Answer choices and answer key.
- Mathematical notation, diagrams, and images.
- Optional questions and alternative groups.
- Total marks and expected duration.
- Student-facing readability on desktop and mobile.

Use the available edit action to correct a question, then save and reopen the preview to confirm the change.

## Validate question types

- For objective questions, confirm every option and the selected answer key.
- For descriptive questions, confirm the expected answer and mark allocation.
- For numerical questions, verify notation, units, intermediate requirements, and accepted precision.
- For questions with images, confirm the image belongs to the right item and remains readable.
- For optional or alternative questions, confirm the selection rules and total marks.

## Validate the complete paper

Add the visible question marks and confirm they match the intended total. Check that the paper can be completed in the available time and that instructions do not contradict the question configuration.

After an edit, preview again. A correct edit is not complete until the saved student-facing version shows it.

<Warning>
  Pay special attention to uploaded papers. Extraction can misread symbols, page order, or answer choices even when the source looks clear.
</Warning>

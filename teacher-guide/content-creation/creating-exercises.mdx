# Create a test

Open **Tests**, select **New Test**, and choose a mode.

## Create Test mode

<img src="/images/app/teacher/create-test.png" alt="Create Test mode with class, batch, subject, question count, optional name, date, and chapter." />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the audience">Select class, section, and subject. Later fields can remain unavailable until the earlier selection is complete.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the assessment">Choose the number of questions, optional display name, date, and chapters.</Step>
  <Step title="Open advanced options">Set difficulty, syllabus scope, and question-type distribution when the default mix is not suitable.</Step>
  <Step title="Generate and wait">Submit the request once. Generation can take time; monitor its status in the Tests list.</Step>
</Steps>

<img src="/images/app/teacher/advanced-test-options.png" alt="Advanced test options for difficulty, topic or chapter scope, and question distribution." />

## Upload Paper mode

<img src="/images/app/teacher/upload-test-paper.png" alt="Upload Paper form with assessment details, required question-key PDF, and optional answer-key PDF." />

- Upload the **Question Key** as a PDF.
- Add an **Answer Key** when available; verify extracted answers before grading.
- Use a clear scan with readable text, upright pages, and complete diagrams.
- Do not upload a learner’s answered paper as the master question key.

<Warning>
  Creating or uploading does not replace review. Extraction and generation can misread notation, diagrams, options, or marks.
</Warning>

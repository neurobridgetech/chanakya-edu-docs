Homework rows show a start date and due date. Use **Preview**, **View students**, **Class analysis**, and **More** as you would for tests. Available actions depend on the homework status.

## Upload homework

Select **New Homework**.

<img src="/images/app/teacher/upload-homework.png" alt="Upload Homework form with class, batch, subject, optional name, duration, question key, and answer key." />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose class and section">You must choose a section before its subjects appear.</Step>
  <Step title="Set subject and name">Use a clear optional name that learners can distinguish from other work.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the duration">Choose one week, two weeks, or a custom period.</Step>
  <Step title="Upload the PDFs">Add the required question key and an optional answer key, then submit.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Before uploading, check that the due date allows time for completion and that the PDF is readable on a phone.
</Tip>

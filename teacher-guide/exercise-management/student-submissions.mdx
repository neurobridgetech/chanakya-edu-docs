# Review student submissions

From **Tests** or **Homework**, locate the item and select **View students**.

## What to review

- The number of assigned learners.
- Not started, in-progress, and submitted states.
- Grading or re-check state.
- Published-result availability.
- Individual learner score and completion details when available.

## Recommended workflow

1. Confirm the correct assessment name and section.
2. Compare the submission count with the assigned count.
3. Follow up with learners who have not submitted before the deadline.
4. Open a submitted learner record to review answers and grading.
5. Use re-check or re-grade only when the response, rubric, or extracted work needs another evaluation.
6. Publish results only after the review is complete.

## Separate completion from performance

A missing submission and a low score require different follow-up. Check status before interpreting the score:

| Situation | First response |
| --- | --- |
| Not started | Confirm access, timing, and learner awareness. |
| In progress | Check the deadline before intervening. |
| Submitted, not graded | Wait for grading or review. |
| Graded, not published | Complete teacher review. |
| Published with an unexpected mark | Inspect the learner's answer and rubric. |

## Use re-check carefully

Start a re-check when the answer evidence, extraction, answer key, or rubric needs another evaluation. Do not use it only to make a score higher. Review the changed marks and feedback before publication.

<Warning>
  Re-checking can change marks and analytics. Confirm the learner and assessment before starting it, then review the new result before publication.
</Warning>

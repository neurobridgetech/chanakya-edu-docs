# Student submissions

From a test or homework row, select **View students**.

<img src="/images/app/teacher/student-list-header.png" alt="All Students page header for a selected test with More actions." />

<img src="/images/app/teacher/student-list-search.png" alt="Student submission search and result count." />

<img src="/images/app/teacher/student-list-columns.png" alt="Submission columns for roll number, name, questions attempted, marks, and actions." />

Use search to locate an authorized learner by name, email, or roll number. Compare **Questions Attempted** with **Marks** before interpreting a low score: missing work and incorrect work require different follow-up.

## Learner-level actions

- **View student analysis** opens the learner’s outcome evidence.
- **View submission history** shows their submission attempts and processing history.
- A re-check should be limited to the intended learner and assessment.

## Page-level actions

<img src="/images/app/teacher/student-list-more-actions.png" alt="Submission page actions for Export and Delete submissions." />

- **Export** downloads the submission view for authorized use.
- **Delete submissions** is a high-impact cleanup action. Confirm scope and institutional policy first.

<Warning>
  Submission pages contain personally identifiable learner data. Do not capture or publish full-page screenshots, and do not send exports through unapproved channels.
</Warning>

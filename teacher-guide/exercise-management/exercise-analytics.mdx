# Test analytics

Select **Class analysis** for a graded assessment.

Review:

- Overall score distribution and class average.
- Completion and missing submissions.
- Question-level success and mark patterns.
- Students needing attention.
- Strengths, weak areas, and recommendations.

If a result looks implausible, open the underlying learner answers before changing marks or teaching plans.

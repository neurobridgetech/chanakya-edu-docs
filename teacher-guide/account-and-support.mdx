## Account, security, and support

Open the profile menu at the bottom of the desktop sidebar.

<img src="/images/app/teacher/account-menu.png" alt="Teacher account menu with Give Feedback, Settings, and Logout." />

## Give feedback

<img src="/images/app/teacher/feedback-form.png" alt="Feedback form with experience rating, feedback field, and Submit action." />

Say what you expected, what happened, and which screen or test was involved. Do not paste passwords, full student records, or answer sheets into feedback.

## Change your password

Open **Settings → Security**.

<img src="/images/app/teacher/settings-header.png" alt="Profile and settings header for account details and security." />

<img src="/images/app/teacher/account-security.png" alt="Account security form for changing a password and signing out." />

Use at least eight characters with uppercase and lowercase letters, a number, and a special character. Never reuse a shared or demonstration password for a personal account.

## Sign out

Use **Logout** or **Sign out**, especially on a shared device. Closing the browser tab is not a substitute for signing out.

<Card title="Help" icon="circle-question">
  Open **Help** from the main menu. Include the test name and approximate time of the issue, but do not include private student details unless support asks through a safe school channel.
</Card>
